Our archive spans vintage garments from the 1920s through the 1990s, contemporary and nondescript wardrobe, acclaimed designer and couture labels, and an unrivaled specialty collection of western wear.

Who We Serve

Built for the production.

Music City Wardrobe is a trade resource. We partner with the teams and professionals who dress music, stage, television, and film — sourcing wardrobe by the production, not the piece.

Production Houses & Studios

Film, television, music, and stage productions sourcing wardrobe at scale — from a single scene to a full season.

Stylists

Celebrity, tour, and editorial stylists building looks for stage, press, red carpet, and camera.

Custom & Costume Designers

Designers pulling reference, foundation pieces, and finished garments to realize a character or an era.

Wardrobe Supervisors

Supervisors and coordinators managing continuity, fittings, and returns across a demanding production schedule.

We work with productions and the professionals behind them — not with individual clients, and we don't rent single pieces to the public.
